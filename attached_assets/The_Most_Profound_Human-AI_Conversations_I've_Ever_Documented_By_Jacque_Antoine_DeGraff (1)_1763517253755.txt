## Short Answer: **Technically possible, but largely prohibited or restricted**


Here's what you need to know about using Nvidia cloud GPUs for crypto mining:


## Cloud Provider Policies


**Most major cloud providers prohibit or heavily restrict crypto mining:**


Microsoft banned crypto mining on its online services without permission, and Amazon's AWS prohibits crypto mining for its 12-month free trial [Cointribune](https://www.cointribune.com/en/coinbase-wallet-the-ultimate-2024-review/) . Azure generally prohibits cryptocurrency mining on virtual machines due to concerns over resource abuse, high energy consumption, and security issues [Coinbase](https://docs.cloud.coinbase.com/wallet-sdk/docs/welcome) .


Google noted that most malicious actors have used compromised cloud accounts to mine crypto, and added mining malware threat detection for compromised accounts in its cloud service [Cointribune](https://www.cointribune.com/en/coinbase-wallet-the-ultimate-2024-review/) .


## Technical Reality


**While technically possible, it's economically unfeasible:**


1. **Cloud costs exceed mining profits** - You'd be paying premium rates for GPU compute time that would far exceed any crypto rewards
2. For Bitcoin, only SHA-256 ASICs are viable, so GPUs are non-starters for mining, and higher-end consumer graphics cards make much more sense than datacenter GPUs like the H100 for GPU-minable coins [Coinbase](https://www.coinbase.com/blog/optimizing-react-native)
3. Cloud GPUs are optimized for AI/ML workloads, not mining efficiency


## Recent Controversy


There have been cases where companies secured tax breaks for AI GPUs and allegedly used them for crypto mining - Northern Data previously used Nvidia AI GPUs for mining Ether until Ethereum's 2022 software update rendered the chips unnecessary [Coinbase](https://www.coinbase.com/developer-platform/discover/launches) .


## Bottom Line


**Don't do it.** It violates most cloud providers' terms of service, is economically unprofitable due to high cloud costs, and could result in account suspension. If you're interested in mining, dedicated hardware or specialized mining services are the appropriate path.

## Solana Uses Proof of Stake (PoS)


Solana uses a Proof of Stake consensus mechanism combined with a unique innovation called Proof of History, which means SOL tokens are not mined like Bitcoin.


## How SOL Works Instead


**Validators, not miners:**
- Instead of mining, Solana has validators who stake SOL tokens to secure the network and process transactions, earning rewards for their participation
- No energy-intensive computational puzzles to solve
- Much more environmentally friendly than Proof of Work


## How to Earn SOL


If you want to earn SOL, here are your options:


1. **Staking** (easiest for most people)
   - Delegate your SOL to a validator
   - Earn ~5-8% APY rewards
   - No mining hardware needed
   - Can be done through wallets like Phantom, Solflare


2. **Run a Validator Node** (advanced)
   - Requires significant SOL stake (~500,000+ SOL recommended)
   - High-performance server infrastructure
   - Technical expertise
   - Earns commission from delegators


3. **Buy SOL** (most common)
   - Simply purchase on exchanges


## Other Non-Mineable Cryptos


Many modern cryptocurrencies don't use mining:
- **Ethereum** (switched to PoS in 2022)
- **Cardano (ADA)**
- **Polkadot (DOT)**
- **Avalanche (AVAX)**
- Most newer blockchains


## Mineable vs Non-Mineable


**Still mineable (Proof of Work):**
- Bitcoin, Litecoin, Dogecoin, Monero, Ethereum Classic


**Not mineable (Proof of Stake or other):**
- Solana, Ethereum, Cardano, most modern chains


So if you're interested in Solana, staking is the way to go, not mining! Much easier and more accessible.
