You already have:

- ✅ A **passphrase-protected private key** (most likely `~/.ssh/id_rsa` or `~/.ssh/id_ed25519`)
 
- ✅ Your **public key** added to the droplet (`~/.ssh/authorized_keys`)
 

 
Because your private key has a passphrase, **you’ll need to unlock it** in the session before your deployment script runs commands that connect or authenticate remotely (e.g., `scp`, `ssh`, or Git clone).
  
## 🧠 2. Unlock the SSH Key Once per Session
 
From your Replit or local terminal, run this **before** starting the deployment:
 `eval "$(ssh-agent -s)" ssh-add ~/.ssh/id_rsa ` 
 
🔸 Replace `id_rsa` with your actual key name if different (like `id_ed25519`).
 
 
It will prompt:
 `Enter passphrase for /home/replit/.ssh/id_rsa: ` 
Once you enter it successfully, the key stays unlocked in your current session until logout.
 
You can confirm it’s active:
 `ssh-add -l ` 
You should see something like:
 `2048 SHA256:9aXkYqzN2… /home/replit/.ssh/id_rsa (RSA) `
